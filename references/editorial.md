# Editorial Decisions

## Choose a Reader Problem

Review recent audits and compare central questions as well as titles. For each candidate record intended reader, concrete question, timely trigger, information gain, evidence and selection/rejection reason. Low risk alone is insufficient. Focus on technology/digital life and practical services; adjacent topics are acceptable when reader value is stronger.

## Build an Argument

Lead in roughly 100-150 Chinese characters with a concrete situation, change or question and the benefit of reading. Never invent personal experience, interviews or experiments. Label illustrative scenarios.

Use the structure the argument needs, not a compulsory seven-section template. Supply relevant background, 2-3 evidence-backed angles, reader implications, limits/counterexamples and a conclusion answering the opening question. Distinguish facts, interpretation and predictions. Each major judgment needs a concrete fact/example and an explanation of how it supports the judgment.

Attribute key facts to named institutions and dates in the body; raw links belong in the audit. Verify denominators, populations, dates and uncertainty. Syndicated reports are not independent corroboration. Remove numerical claims that cannot be resolved.

Delete repetition instead of filling a word quota. Review repeated abstract expressions such as 真正稀缺、值得关注、长期能力 in context. If replacing a few nouns makes the article fit an unrelated event, rewrite substantively.

## Title Selection

After drafting, create five candidates with different angles: a reader question, practical consequence, concrete change, explanatory comparison or evidence-backed judgment. These are possibilities, not fixed templates. Use concrete objects; stay within the live platform limit (currently observed 2-30 characters).

For each candidate record 0-5 scores and brief reasons for clarity, reader relevance, specificity, honest curiosity and body delivery. These are editorial heuristics, not predicted click rates. Reject factual distortion and undelivered promises regardless of total. Revise the opening/body if the chosen title shifts the focus.

Example: 科普不只给答案，更要展示答案怎样得来 is abstract. AI回答很流畅，为什么仍要核对来源？ works only when the body centers on AI source-checking with usable examples. Do not attach it to an unchanged general science-month essay. Avoid invented numbers, withheld essential facts, guaranteed outcomes and exaggerated urgency.

## Images and Review

Prefer sourced data charts, labeled comparisons, explanatory workflows and relevant scenes. Generated examples are illustrations, not actual event photographs. Check image labels and data. A generic meeting image does not add information simply because the subject matches.

Record each review as {status: pass|fail|pending, evidence: ...}. Required reviews: single_question, reader_benefit, evidence, original_analysis, fact_comment_separation, freshness, no_filler, title_delivery, images, compliance. Cite concrete passages/observations, not blanket booleans. File checks cannot establish these judgments.

Revise at most twice before skipping the run with a reason. Keep topic/title decisions and failure reasons for later improvement.
