# Compliance Notes

Use these notes to reduce publishing risk for 今日头条-style news drafts. This is not legal advice and platform rules can change; verify current platform rules for high-risk posts.

## Safer Editorial Style

- Use factual, sourced, neutral wording.
- Attribute claims to named sources.
- Avoid definitive judgments when facts are still developing.
- Avoid sensational labels, insults, mockery, rumor framing, or emotional amplification.
- Avoid calls to attack, harass, boycott, invest, buy medicine, or imitate risky behavior.
- Name the actual source and date. Do not hide missing evidence behind "多家媒体报道".

## High-Risk Topic Filters

Mark as `high` and consider omitting:

- politics, ideology, national security, military, diplomacy, ethnic or religious conflict
- violent crime, suicide, self-harm, terrorism, weapons, gore
- minors, school incidents, personal privacy, doxxing, leaked records
- unverified rumors, celebrity scandals without reliable confirmation
- medical treatment claims, miracle cures, financial advice, investment promises
- legal cases where facts are disputed or trial details are sensitive
- disasters or deaths when the draft would look like traffic exploitation

Mark as `medium` and rewrite carefully:

- public disputes, brand controversies, workplace conflict
- regulatory investigations, recalls, safety incidents
- consumer complaints, lawsuits, layoffs
- social incidents with unclear facts

Generally safer:

- technology/product launches, sports results, entertainment releases, culture, travel, lifestyle, public service information, weather alerts from official sources, education tips that avoid sensitive claims.

## Rewriting Pattern

Review meaning in context rather than replacing words. Verify the exact claim and rewrite the whole sentence using concrete facts and named attribution. Do not turn "翻车" into "引发关注" or invent a hot-list appearance. Keyword matches are review hints; an empty scan does not prove accuracy or compliance. Remove unsupported accusations rather than laundering them through anonymous attribution.

If a rewrite would hide a prohibited or harmful meaning, omit the item instead of trying to disguise it.

## Final Review Checklist

- Each factual claim has a source or clear attribution.
- No private personal data is included.
- No graphic or exploitative image is used.
- No medical, financial, legal, or safety instruction is framed as advice.
- The title matches the body and image.
- The draft can stand alone if manually copied into 今日头条.
